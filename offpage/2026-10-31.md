## 2. LinkedIn post (from the guide "Woven vs nonwoven geotextile")

Post from the Kiran Nonwovens company page once it exists, or from Ranjeet Sharma's own
profile until then. Add one photo of a geotextile roll.

> Woven or nonwoven geotextile? They are not interchangeable.
>
> ▸ Woven geotextile is thin and strong in tension. Nonwoven (needle punched) is thick and
>   felt-like, and lets water pass through it and along it.
> ▸ Use nonwoven for drainage, filtration, liner protection and separation on wet ground.
>   Use woven or geogrid for reinforcement over very soft ground.
> ▸ The same GSM does not make them equal. Compare the strength, puncture, opening size
>   and permeability your design needs.
>
> We make nonwoven geotextile only, so this is the question we get asked first.
> Full guide with a comparison table:
> https://kirannonwovens.com/guides/woven-vs-nonwoven-geotextile
>
