## 2. LinkedIn post (from "Geotextile test methods explained")

From the company page once it exists, or from Ranjeet Sharma's profile until then.
Photo: a geotextile roll or a sample being weighed.

> A geotextile specification is a list of test values, not a weight.
>
> ▸ Grab tensile (N) and wide-width tensile (kN/m) are different tests. They cannot be
>   converted into each other by a fixed factor.
> ▸ AOS (ASTM, dry sieving) and O90 (ISO, wet sieving) both describe pore size, but give
>   different numbers on the same roll.
> ▸ A "typical value" on a datasheet is not a MARV. If your tender lists minimum values,
>   compare like with like.
>
> We wrote a plain-language guide to every common test and how to read a lab report:
> https://kirannonwovens.com/guides/geotextile-test-methods-explained
>
> #geotextile #geosynthetics #civilengineering #qualitycontrol #infrastructure
