## Rules
- Never pay for a link or offer money for a listing in an article. Paid directory listings are
  fine only if they are clearly ads (and then the link should be nofollow/sponsored).
- No link exchanges ("you link me, I link you") as a scheme.
- Do not claim we are the manufacturer; say "supplier", "exporter", "made at our partner plant".
- After any listing goes live, send Claude the URL: it goes into offpage/listings.md and the
  Organization `sameAs` on the site.
