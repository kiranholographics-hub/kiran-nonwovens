# SEO master strategy: India + world (Kiran Nonwovens)

Likha: 23 Oct 2026. Roz ka kaam [SEO-ROUTINE.md](SEO-ROUTINE.md) mein hai; ye file batati hai
**kya, kyun aur kis order mein**. Sach baat pehle: koi bhi SEO "world mein #1" ki guarantee
nahi de sakta. Jo kar sakte hain: sahi keywords chunna, har mahine authority badhana, aur
6–12 mahine mein un searches par page one aana jahan buyer sach mein order deta hai.

---

## 1. Abhi site kahan khadi hai (audit)

**Technical base pehle se strong hai.** Isme bada kaam baaki nahi:
- Har route prerender hota hai (Google ko poora HTML milta hai, khali React div nahi).
- Har page ka apna title, description, canonical, OG/Twitter tags.
- Schema: Organization, WebSite, Product, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, Article, LocalBusiness (/india).
- sitemap.xml (git ke asli lastmod ke saath), robots.txt, IndexNow, Search Console verified.
- Fonts preload, video ke liye webp poster.
- Content: 20 product pages, 21 guides, 6 export region pages, /india page.

**Asli kami authority ki hai, technical ki nahi.** "nonwoven manufacturer India" jaise head
keywords par IndiaMART, JustDial, TradeIndia aur 10–20 saal purani sites hain. Unhe sirf
on-page se nahi hara sakte; links, brand mentions, reviews aur asli photos chahiye.

**Jo cheezein ranking rok rahi hain (aapki taraf se chahiye):**
1. Asli plant / product photos aur video (abhi placeholder). Google Images + trust dono ke liye.
2. Certifications / test reports PDF (geotextile buyers aur tenders yahi maangte hain).
3. MOQ, sample policy, lead time (buyer ke sawaal; FAQ aur schema mein jayenge).
4. Google Business Profile, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, LinkedIn ke live URLs (Organization schema
   ke `sameAs` mein daalne hain; bina URL ke hum kuch nahi likhte).

## 2. Keyword strategy: teen layer

| Layer | Example | Kab tak | Kaise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Long-tail niche (India)** | geotextile for canal lining India, bag filter felt cement plant India | 1–3 mahine | Daily niche guide (chal raha hai) |
| **B. Export "from India"** | nonwoven felt supplier from India to UAE, geotextile exporter India Africa | 3–6 mahine | Region pages + "from India" angle + B2B portals |
| **C. Head terms** | needle punched felt manufacturer India, nonwoven geotextile manufacturer | 6–12+ mahine | Authority: links, listings, reviews, PR |

Rule: Layer A se traffic aur trust banta hai, wahi internal links se Layer C ke pages ko
taakat deta hai. Isliye har guide product page aur pillar page ko link karta hai.

**World ke liye sabse samajhdaar raasta:** overseas buyer "supplier USA" search karta hai to
local distributor milta hai. Indian exporter ka mauka hai in searches mein:
- "<product> manufacturer India" / "from India" / "Indian supplier"
- HS code searches (5602.10, 5603.14 etc.): importers aur sourcing agents
- Technical sawaal ("what GSM geotextile for road", "needle punched vs thermal bonded"):
  ye duniya bhar se aate hain aur competition kam hai.

## 3. 90 din ka plan

### Mahina 1: Foundation + quick wins
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** (Jaipur address, category "Nonwoven fabric manufacturer" ya
      jo sabse kareeb mile), photos, har hafte ek post. "Near me" aur Maps ke liye sabse bada lever.
- [ ] **Bing Webmaster Tools** (Search Console se import, 5 min). Bing = ChatGPT/Copilot search ka base.
- [ ] **NAP same everywhere**: naam, address, phone bilkul ek jaisa (LISTINGS.md ki copy).
- [ ] Profiles: IndiaMART, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, Alibaba (agar budget ho), LinkedIn
      company page, YouTube channel. Har jagah website link.
- [ ] Profile URLs Claude ko do → Organization schema mein `sameAs` (brand knowledge panel ke liye).
- [ ] Asli photos (har product ki 2–3, plant ki 5–10) → `IMAGES_READY = true`.
      File naam aur alt text keyword wale honge (Claude karega).
- [ ] Search Console: Performance report ka screenshot har Shukravar.

### Mahina 2: Content depth
- [ ] Daily niche guide queue khatam karna (landfill/mining, automotive OEM, luggage,
      packaging, flooring underlay, Hindi test page).
- [ ] Har product page par: application table, GSM chunne ka tareeka, 5–8 asli FAQ,
      comparison (needle punched vs thermal bonded, PP vs polyester geotextile).
- [ ] **Comparison / "vs" guides** (global searches, kam competition):
      PP vs polyester geotextile, woven vs nonwoven geotextile, needle punched vs spunbond
      (honestly: hum spunbond nahi banate).
- [ ] **HS code page** ek hi jagah, sahi aur saaf (importers ke liye).
- [ ] Har guide mein ek downloadable spec sheet / GSM chart (PDF): link magnet + lead.

### Mahina 3: Authority (links + mentions)
- [ ] **Industry directories**: ANFA / INDA member list (agar member ho), Textile Excellence,
      Fibre2Fashion company listing, Technical Textiles India portals.
- [ ] **Export bodies**: TEXPROCIL / SRTEPC / FIEO member directory (agar member ho): .org/.gov
      link, bahut strong.
- [ ] **Guest article / PR**: Fibre2Fashion, Textile Value Chain, Technical Textiles
      International: ek technical article (e.g. "geotextile GSM selection for Indian roads").
- [ ] **Trade fairs**: Techtextil (Frankfurt / India), Heimtextil: exhibitor list mein website link.
- [ ] **Customer / dealer sites**: jo buyer khush hain unse "supplier" page par link maangna.
- [ ] **YouTube**: 30–60 sec videos (machine chalti hui, roll ka GSM test, sample packing).
      Video Google search mein alag se dikhte hain aur trust badhate hain.

## 4. India vs World: alag tareeka

**India (domestic):**
- Google Business Profile + reviews (sabse zaroori). Har khush buyer se review maango.
- IndiaMART / TradeIndia par active rehna (inke pages khud Google par rank karte hain;
  wahan se bhi enquiry aati hai).
- Hindi content dheere dheere (pehle ek test page; chale to aur).
- Government tender words: "MoRTH", "NHAI", "PMGSY" ke saath geotextile guides
  (hamesha "current edition apne engineer se confirm karein").

**World (export):**
- Site English mein hi rahegi; har country ke liye alag bhasha abhi nahi (patla content nuksaan karta hai).
  Arabic sirf tab, jab GCC se sach mein enquiry aaye.
- Region pages par us region ke asli projects/use-cases (jab aap batao).
- B2B marketplaces: Alibaba / Global Sources / TradeKey. Overseas buyer pehle wahan dhundhta hai.
- LinkedIn par procurement managers tak post + direct outreach (SEO ke saath sabse fast lead).
- Google Search Console mein "Countries" filter: jahan impressions aa rahe hain wahi double down.

## 5. AI search (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews)

2026 mein buyer AI se bhi poochta hai "best needle punched felt supplier India". Inme aane ke liye:
- Saaf, factual, table wala content (spec, GSM, width): AI isi ko quote karta hai. (Ho raha hai.)
- Brand ka naam doosri sites par (directories, articles, LinkedIn): AI ko "mentions" chahiye.
- Bing Webmaster verified (ChatGPT search Bing index use karta hai).
- robots.txt mein AI crawlers block nahi hain; aise hi rehne do.

## 6. Kya NAHI karna (penalty / paisa barbaad)
- Paid backlinks, PBN, "1000 links ₹999" wale offers: Google penalty.
- Har country ke liye copy-paste pages (pehle 32 the, 6 region pages mein badle; sahi kiya).
- Fake reviews, fake certifications, fake client names.
- Competitors ke naam se "Top 10" list.
- Keyword stuffing ya hidden text.
