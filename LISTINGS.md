Kiran Nonwovens supplies export buyers with needle punched and thermal bonded nonwoven felt, automotive felt, filtration felt and nonwoven geotextile from India.

Typical uses: automotive NVH and trims, geotextile for roads, drainage and pipeline protection, filter felt, footwear and apparel interlining, packaging and protective felt.
