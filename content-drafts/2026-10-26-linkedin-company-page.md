## First post (draft)

What GSM nonwoven geotextile does a road project need?

There is no single number. GSM tells you the weight of the fabric per square metre, but the right grade depends on the subgrade soil, the size of the stone placed on it, the traffic, and how the fabric is installed. That is why the project design sets it, not a catalogue.

We wrote a plain guide on what GSM tells you, what it does not, and what else belongs in a geotextile specification:
https://kirannonwovens.com/guides/geotextile-gsm-for-road-construction-india

We make needle punched nonwoven geotextile from 100 to 1200 GSM, in rolls up to 5.2 m wide, to the specification you send.
