**Company description** (written differently from the IndiaMART text on purpose, so the two
directory profiles are not copies of each other):

Kiran Nonwovens is a Jaipur-based supplier of made-to-order needle punched and thermal bonded nonwovens for project buyers, component makers and importers.

What buyers order from us: non woven geotextile in PP or polyester for roads, railways, drains, canals and landfills; automotive felt for NVH, insulation and interior parts; filter felt for dust collectors; and felt for footwear, garments, luggage, packaging, carpet backing and underlay.

Each order is built to the buyer's specification: 100 to 1200 GSM, roll widths of 5.0 to 5.2 m or slit narrower, in polyester, polypropylene (virgin or recycled), viscose or blends, with thickness, roll length and colour set to the job. Material is produced at the plant of our manufacturing partner, Miracle Nonwoven Industries.

To get a quote, share the application, GSM, width, quantity and delivery location. Samples are available on request.

Buyer guides: https://kirannonwovens.com/guides
